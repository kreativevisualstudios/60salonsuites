Privacy

Privacy Practices Related to Customer Data, Effective October 2024

At 60 Salon Suites, we understand your information is important and are committed to protecting personal information provided to us. This Privacy Policy describes privacy practices that apply when you use our websites, mobile applications, or platforms that link or refer to 60 Salon Suites or a 60 Salon Suites franchise location.

What Information Is Collected?

We may collect personal information from customers or suite owners of 60 Salon Suites, including information voluntarily provided through our websites, app, or social media pages. This may include a name, address, telephone number, email address, industry, or other personal identifier. We may also collect payment information to process financial transactions that have been requested.

How Is Personal Information Used?

We collect information about suite owners and customers to conduct business and provide services. Uses may include:

  • Sending or initiating direct marketing programs.
  • Contacting customers if an issue needs to be resolved.
  • Learning more about our customers and tenants.
  • Understanding device usage or unique identifiers, such as operating system versions and settings used to access our site.

Third-Party Websites

The information practices of websites linked from our website that are not affiliated with 60 Salon Suites are not covered by this Privacy Policy. We are not responsible for third-party policies or practices. These sites may use cookies or collect data or personal information in ways we cannot control. Location microsites within the 60 Salon Suites website are covered by this policy.

Choices and Consent

60 Salon Suites will not use or share personal information in ways incompatible with the purposes described in this policy. Where applicable law requires express consent or opt-in for certain types of information, we will use processes designed to obtain that consent. Communications will include instructions for opting out or unsubscribing when applicable.

Phone numbers collected for SMS consent will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.

Customers and tenants may choose not to provide personal information, limit the information provided, or withdraw consent. You may request removal of your contact information from marketing lists, including email, text, and direct mail marketing, by contacting us at 60salonsuites@60salonsuites.com or through our website.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may modify this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page or communicated by other appropriate means and become effective when the updated policy is posted.
